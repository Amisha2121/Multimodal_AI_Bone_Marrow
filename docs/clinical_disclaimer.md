# Clinical & Research Disclaimer

## This is a Research Prototype

This system — "An Agentic Multimodal Explainable RAG Framework for Personalized Bone Marrow Assessment" — is a **research prototype** developed for academic purposes. It has **not** been validated for clinical use, regulatory approval, or deployment in any medical setting.

## Not Medical Advice

- Outputs generated by this system (including diagnostic suggestions, cell-differential estimates, and literature-supported assessments) do **not** constitute medical advice, a clinical diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation.
- No output from this system should be acted upon without independent review by a qualified haematologist or licensed medical professional.
- This system is not a substitute for laboratory testing, pathological review, or clinical judgement.

## Data Sources

- **Vision model:** Trained on the Matek et al. 2021 Bone Marrow Cytomorphology dataset (TCIA, CC BY 4.0). Performance is bounded by the characteristics of this training data. Generalisation to other staining protocols, scanner types, or patient populations is not guaranteed.
- **Knowledge base:** Derived from publicly available open-access literature and WHO guideline summaries. It may not reflect the most current clinical guidelines at the time of use.
- **Patient data:** All patient case data used in this research is sourced exclusively from the BoneMarrowWSI-PediatricLeukemia open-access cohort (NCI IDC). Complete Blood Count (CBC), cytogenetics, and molecular genetics data are absent from this dataset and are represented as `null` throughout the pipeline.

## Classification Standard

This system uses the **WHO Classification of Haematolymphoid Tumours, 5th edition (2022)** as its primary diagnostic reference. Clinical practice may differ by institution or country.

## Limitations

- Cell classification performance may degrade on rare cell classes (< 1% prevalence) and on cells from patients with conditions not well-represented in the training dataset.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation can produce plausible-sounding but incorrect or outdated information. The Critic agent reduces but does not eliminate this risk.
- Abstention thresholds are calibrated on a small hand-built evaluation set and may not generalise to all query types.

## Intended Use

This system is intended for:
- Academic research and education in haematology and medical AI
- Demonstration of agentic RAG architectures in biomedical domains
- Ablation studies and evaluation of explainability methods

It is **not** intended for clinical deployment, patient management, or any use that could directly affect patient care.

## Ethics & Institutional Compliance

Users of this system are responsible for ensuring compliance with their institution's ethics and IRB requirements before using it in any research study involving patient data.
